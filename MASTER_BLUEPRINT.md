# ZARION Master Blueprint

## Combat contract
Responsive cancel windows; launch/juggle/air combat; weapon/style switching; lock-on/free targeting; dodge/parry; resource/transformation; enemy hit reactions; scoring/style rank; training mode; deterministic move data; camera designed for crowds/bosses.

## Unreal
Ultra/Narrative foundation. Compare Flexible Combat vs Action RPG and select one ownership core, then customize heavily. Movement and boss assets accelerate prototypes, never dictate feel. Combat data tables/specs become canonical and testable.

## AXIOM
Port move/state/cancel/hitbox/resource contracts, not Unreal Blueprint internals. Automated combat fixtures plus feel/playtest targets establish parity.

## XYRA
Frame/cancel tests; input latency; animation/root-motion; hitbox/hurtbox; camera; enemy crowd behavior; boss exploit testing; save/progression; performance under VFX/crowds; controller remap/accessibility.

## Delivery
Combat lab → movement/camera → enemy AI → boss → vertical slice → weapons/styles/progression → campaign/content → ranking/challenges → optimization → AXIOM.
